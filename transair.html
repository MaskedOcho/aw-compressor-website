About Transair

The Trusted Name In Modular Piping

Transair® is the original modular piping system utilizing aluminum pipe and quick-connect fittings, launched in 1996. With over 750,000 global installations and more than 25 years of product innovation, Transair has become the trusted brand for compressed air, vacuum, inert gas, industrial water and oil networks.

Transair aluminum compressed air piping can be installed up to 4x faster than traditional threaded or welded piping systems, with minimal tooling and no specialized labor required.
