About Us

Our Story

Four Decades Of Honest Service

A&W Compressor & Mechanical Services was founded in 1982 with a simple goal: give East Tennessee businesses a compressed-air partner they could actually trust. What started as a small local repair operation has grown into a full-service provider covering sales, rental, maintenance and system audits, without losing the personal, honest approach we started with.

Today our team supports industrial plants, medical and dental practices, automotive shops and homeowners alike, backed by factory-trained technicians and 24/7 emergency response.
