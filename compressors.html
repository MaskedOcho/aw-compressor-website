Condition

New, Used, Refurbished & Remanufactured

New

Brand-new units straight from the manufacturer, sized and specified for your exact application, with full factory warranty.

Used

Quality inspected used compressors, a cost-effective option for backup capacity or budget-conscious operations.

Refurbished

Cleaned, tested and serviced units that deliver near-new reliability at a fraction of the cost of new equipment.

Remanufactured

Rebuilt to original specifications with new wear components, ideal when you need factory-grade performance on a budget.

Airend Replacement

Airend failure doesn't always mean a new compressor. We stock and install replacement airends for most major brands.

Variable Speed

Variable speed drive compressors that match output to demand in real time, cutting energy costs significantly.

Power Source & Format

Electric, Diesel & Portable Options

Electric Compressors

Rotary screw, reciprocating and oil-free electric compressors for facilities with reliable grid power. Quiet, efficient and low-maintenance.

Diesel Compressors

Job-site and remote-location diesel units that run independent of grid power, built for tough conditions and mobile applications.

Portable Compressors

Trailer-mounted and skid units for construction, sandblasting and field service work, available for sale or rental.
