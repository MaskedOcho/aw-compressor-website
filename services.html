Services

Repair, rental, maintenance and compressed air audits, keeping your system reliable.

Repair

From emergency breakdowns to routine troubleshooting, our factory-trained technicians diagnose and repair rotary screw, reciprocating, and oil-free compressors of every major brand, on-site or in our shop.

  • Airend rebuilds & replacement
  • Electrical & controls diagnostics
  • Leak detection & repair
  • Emergency 24/7 response

Rental

Whether it's a planned shutdown, seasonal demand spike or equipment failure, our rental fleet of electric and diesel compressors keeps your operation running while your primary unit is serviced or replaced.

  • Short and long-term rental terms
  • Electric & diesel portable units
  • Delivery & setup available
  • Rent-to-own options

Maintenance

Scheduled preventative maintenance plans reduce unplanned downtime and extend the life of your equipment. We tailor service intervals to your compressor's duty cycle and manufacturer specs.

  • Oil & filter changes
  • Belt & separator inspections
  • Custom maintenance contracts
  • Detailed service records

Compressed Air Audit

Leaks, poor system design, and inefficient equipment can waste 20-30% of the energy your compressed air system consumes. Our audits identify exactly where you're losing money and how to fix it.

  • Leak detection survey
  • Energy usage analysis
  • System design review
  • Written recommendations report
